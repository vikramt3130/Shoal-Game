    BIGGER RISKS.

    The number above each fish is its mass. Eat lower-mass fish and avoid higher-mass fish. Special glowing fish grant Gold Shield (ungulpable for 5s), Sword Speed (3× swim for 7s, but still vulnerable to bigger fish), or Giant Gulp (2.5× size and gulp power for 10s). Buff timers appear under your mass. Evolutions happen at mass 50 (Goldfish), 200 (Puffer), 500 (Baby Shark), 1,000 (Shark), and 2,000 (Whale).

    Getting eaten resets your growth, but you return after a short pause with temporary protection. There’s no match timer and no final round.

    Use WASD or arrow keys on desktop. On touch screens, drag the joystick. The ocean has visible edges. Turn around when you reach one.
